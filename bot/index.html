KavaCrawlBot

KavaCrawlBot is the crawler behind KavaCrawl, a free app that helps people find kava bars and compare their menus and prices. It identifies itself with this User-Agent:

KavaCrawlBot/1.0 (+https://kavacrawl.app/bot)

What it fetches

It visits the website of a kava bar or retailer that is listed in KavaCrawl and reads the public menu page, or the menu image or PDF linked from it, to record item names and prices. It does not log in, submit forms, or read anything behind a login. It only fetches a bar's own site, not search engines or social networks.

How often and how fast

  • About once a week, and only when the page has changed.
  • At least 2 seconds between requests to the same site. If your robots.txt sets a Crawl-delay, it waits that long instead (up to 30 seconds).
  • A small number of pages per site: the home page and a few likely menu locations.

Robots.txt and how to block it

KavaCrawlBot reads robots.txt before fetching and does not crawl paths it disallows. To stop it completely, add:

User-agent: KavaCrawlBot
Disallow: /

Sites that block automated access are not retried repeatedly. Bar owners who would rather supply their menu directly can do so in the KavaCrawl app.
