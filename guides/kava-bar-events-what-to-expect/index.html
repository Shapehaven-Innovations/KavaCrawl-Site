Kava bar events: trivia, open mic and what to expect

Last updated October 8, 2026

Many kava bars run regular event nights, such as trivia, open mic, live music and game nights. Schedules differ from bar to bar and change often, so check the bar's own page or an app on the day you go.

Common event nights

NightWhat it usually is
TriviaTeams answer rounds of questions. It is a good night to bring a group; ask the bar how teams sign up.
Open micLocal musicians, poets and comedians take a turn on stage. Come to listen or to perform.
Live musicAcoustic sets and bands. The room is usually busier and louder than a regular night.
Game nightsBoard games, cards, pool or corn hole, depending on the venue's space.
Community meetupsRegulars and newcomers gathering around a shared interest, from book clubs to study groups.

Every bar is different. Details such as sign-up, cover charges and start times are set by each venue, so ask the bar rather than assuming.

How to find out what is on tonight

  1. Check the bar's own page or social account. That is where a venue posts its regular schedule and any one-off changes.
  2. Use KavaCrawl. It lists what is happening at bars near you, so you can compare options without opening a dozen pages.
  3. Call ahead. Hours and event schedules change, and a quick call avoids a wasted trip.

If it is your first time

  • Ask the staff. Kava bars are social spaces, and the people behind the counter are used to walking newcomers through the menu and how ordering works.
  • Go with other people. Event nights are built around conversation, so a group is a good way to start.
  • Arrive a little early for popular nights. Seating can fill up when there is an event.
  • Mind the room. On trivia or music nights the space gets lively, so pick a table that suits how much you want to talk.

Plan a night around more than one stop

If there are a few bars near each other, you can turn an evening into a crawl: pick the stops, decide the order, and check each venue's hours and any event times before you leave. Allow time to get between bars, and plan how you will get home. KavaCrawl has a route builder for this, so you can set the order of your stops and start.

Event schedules change often. Treat this guide as a general picture of what these nights are like and confirm details with the venue.
