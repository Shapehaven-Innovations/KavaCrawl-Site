Method

Counts are taken from the venue records in the KavaCrawl app database on October 9, 2026. Only venues the app found through kava searches are counted, so tea, coffee and other venues in the database are excluded. Being found by a kava search does not confirm a venue serves kava. Each venue is assigned to the city and state or province in its listed address. Venue names are not published here.

Counts reflect what is in the app, not every kava bar that exists, and they change as venues are added or removed. A city with a low count may simply have fewer listings so far.
