How the Index works

TL;DR Every bar is scored from 0 to 100 on up to seven factors, with price weighing the most. A bar without enough data shows Not yet scored rather than a guess.

What it looks at

The KavaCrawl Index scores each bar from 0 to 100 on price and value, using up to seven things.

  • Price counts most
  • Menu
  • Hours
  • Happy hour
  • Dog policy
  • Data freshness
  • Quiet counts least

The price is compared with other bars in the same metro area, or a wider area when too few nearby bars have a price. Freshness is how recently the bar's data was checked, and quiet is how calm it tends to be.

When a bar gets a score

A known price+ plus 3 or more other known details= gives Index score
Anything less= gives Not yet scored

Not yet scored means we don't have enough data yet, not that the bar is bad. Quiet doesn't count toward the details needed.

  • Never counted as zero. Details we don't know are left out, and the rest are re-weighted.
  • Kept fresh. Menus are refreshed weekly, and in the app a shell price older than 60 days is marked as possibly out of date.
  • Bars, never people. Nobody can pay to win a comparison, and ads never change it.
  • Make it yours. In the app you can change how much each factor counts and share a comparison made with your weights.
